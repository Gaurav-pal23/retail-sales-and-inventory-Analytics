🛍️ Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project designed to analyze retail sales performance, inventory levels, product trends, and business KPIs.

The dashboard helps businesses understand their sales performance, identify top-performing products, monitor inventory, and make data-driven decisions to improve overall business efficiency.

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

Analyze overall retail sales performance.
Track important business KPIs.
Identify top-selling and underperforming products.
Analyze sales trends over time.
Monitor inventory levels.
Identify products with low or high stock.
Understand category-wise and product-wise performance.
Provide actionable insights for better inventory and sales management.
